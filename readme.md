## OrienteeringVR
Take on the role of orienteer and try your navigational skills in this skill-demanding game. You will need to master your compass and map reading skills in order to become the fastest one to complete the route. 

### Learn how to do orienteering
With the beginer-friendly tutorial, you can easily learn how to efficiently move through the forest.

### Try to find yourself in the woods
Do you think that you are good in navigation? Take your beliefs to the test and find out if they are correct.

### Test your skills in the night
Running during the day is too easy? Try the night mode and rediscover the harder versions of the terrains.

### Map generation
Test yourself on the real life maps or make your own! You can generate maps that are made in OpenOrienteering Mapper in scale 1:10000 with ISOM2017-2 symbol set.
